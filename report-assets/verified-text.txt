Statistical reading
New paired intervals: 20,000 percentile bootstrap draws of correctness differences, stratified by true class
(fine subtype for CIFAR-100). Fixes and losses are computed on the same images; p is the two-sided exact
McNemar test. These exploratory p-values are not adjusted for multiple comparisons.



Anchor and reranking plots use the paired bootstrap intervals saved with their original reports. Image-level
intervals do not capture variation from retraining seeds, prompt choices, split construction, or repeated
development-set use.



Next hypothesis: SigLIP 2-only nomination finds correct examples that CLIP never nominates. Compare it with
CLIP-only, plus the rerank control, at matched realized quotas; check class coverage, training-seed
repeatability and adaptation time before student retraining.

APPENDIX / EXPERIMENT INVENTORY


Baseline, scale, and what fails
Hypotheses, measured results, and failure modes; stages and splits are kept separate.



 HYPOTHESIS
 EuroSAT small pipeline and 160/320-image sweep: more data or more anchors should help.

 RESULT
 160-image trace: 35 candidates → 34 distinct anchors, 25 correct; Herbaceous had no CLIP top-1 candidates and
 needed fallback. In the separate four-cell sweep, CDE was 117/240, DINO 142–159/240, student 81–140/240.
 Raising the cap from 4 to 12 added anchors, reduced precision, improved the teacher in both matched rows, and
 hurt the student. At 320 images one class lost coverage.

 INTERPRETATION
 More images or anchors do not guarantee class coverage; fallback and deduplication matter. Higher teacher
 accuracy did not automatically reach the student.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Larger small-data hyperparameter grid: tune head and student to remove the gap.

 RESULT
 10 of 128 configurations are saved, 20 epoch checkpoints. Best saved student 143/240, tied with its teacher;
 students ranged from 59 to 143 correct.

 INTERPRETATION
 Partial, single-seed grid; no defensible global winner. Epochs and batch size also change the number of
 updates.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Instrumented EuroSAT CPU replay and DINO-space audit: find the specific failure.

 RESULT
 On 240 validation images: CDE 117, teacher 134, student 87 at epoch 1 and 124 at epoch 4. Fresh 240-image
 subset: 130, 137, and 121 at epoch 4. Herbaceous: 24 true train examples, zero top-1 nominations, one wrong
 fallback, 0/16 validation correct throughout. Pasture had no correct anchors; Permanent Crop had four wrong
 anchors. Two of nine supported predicted-class DINO regions lacked anchors; only 3/7 and 1/4 of their rows had
 correct CLIP labels.

 INTERPRETATION
 An absent nomination is different from low anchor precision. Pure diversity may give more slots to coherent
 *wrong* labels. At epoch 4 the student fixed 24 teacher errors and lost 34 correct teacher predictions.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Full-split EuroSAT local release-style baseline: is NoLA itself viable at scale?

 RESULT
 CDE 51.09%, teacher 67.09%, best student 76.69% validation (epoch 3); selected checkpoint 76.27% test
 (6,178/8,100). Epoch 50 fell to 71.13% validation.

 INTERPRETATION
 End-to-end NoLA worked well in this run. Checkpoint selection mattered; small pilots that lost to DINO do not
 establish that the student generally fails. Local release-style run, not an exact multi-seed paper
 reproduction.

Text-side experiments (CDE)
Hypotheses, measured results, and failure modes; stages and splits are kept separate.



 HYPOTHESIS
 Three prompt prototypes via K-means instead of one class mean.

 RESULT
 EuroSAT 240-image pilot: mean 118, log-mean-exp (LME) 127, max 103; Herbaceous 0/16 in all arms.

 INTERPRETATION
 Soft pooling looked promising on a reused small set; max was worse, consistent with an individual mismatched
 prompt dominating. No evidence of rare-class rescue.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Compress authored prompts via complete-link cosine; use SVD as a geometry diagnostic.

 RESULT
 On the other 5,160 EuroSAT validation images: mean 53.59%; unmerged 100 descriptions 54.28%; 36/57/72 merged
 prototypes 48.90/48.39/49.42%. SVD projection marked some pairs as close that failed full-space cosine; they
 were not merged on that basis.

 INTERPRETATION
 Merging can alter both representation and per-description weights; the losses do not isolate information loss.
 SVD directions are not subtype counts.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Broad descriptions plus named or unnamed variations should recover semantic coverage.

 RESULT
 Same 5,160 rows: broad-only 47.67%, best broad-plus-variants 50.21%, below 53.59% mean and 54.28% unmerged
 controls.

 INTERPRETATION
 Adding variants helped that weak broad bank, not the strongest matched prompt controls. Explicit class naming
 was not a guarantee of improvement.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Merge the released EuroSAT bank rather than the newly authored bank.

 RESULT
 Same 5,160 rows: original released CDE 51.10%; descriptions-only centroid 50.79%; unmerged released
 descriptions 51.96%; best merge (0.80) 52.29%, 0.75 merge 52.15%. Herbaceous still had zero correct.

 INTERPRETATION
 Some prompt compression worked for this bank, but the result did not transfer across banks or solve the main
 class failure.

Text-side experiments (CDE)
Continued from the previous page.



 HYPOTHESIS
 Photo-wrap each description, then cluster at 0.75 and use in NoLA.

 RESULT
 EuroSAT corrected Colab arm (16 prototypes): teacher 3,624→3,679/5,400; student epoch 1 +1.15 pp and epoch 2
 −0.30 pp. No verified final test result for this comparison. RESISC45: 50 descriptions/class → 123 total
 prototypes; frozen CDE 58.03→56.05%.

 INTERPRETATION
 Student advantage reversed by epoch; across datasets even the CDE effect reversed. An earlier unwrapped
 author-scale arm was superseded, not a completed comparison.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Keep the mean and add only sufficiently different clusters with separate LME components.

 RESULT
 Wrapped-bank 0.75 gate retained 0/16 EuroSAT clusters and 0/27 CIFAR-100 coarse clusters. Exact mean parity:
 51.56% and 58.50%. CIFAR gate 0.90 activated 7 clusters: 58.36% (−0.14 pp, interval includes zero).

 INTERPRETATION
 An inactive gate tests the mean, not active variants. More permissive gating did not show a gain on that CIFAR
 bank.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Append the class template to each description, with a single mean vector.

 RESULT
 Frozen CDE: Oxford Pets 85.61→87.90% (3,669, our one-template control); EuroSAT 51.06→51.48%; RESISC45
 58.07→58.86%; CIFAR-100 coarse 58.50→50.48% versus previous photo-wrapped prompts.

 INTERPRETATION
 Favorable for some domain/breed wording; not a universal formatting rule. CIFAR also changed wording and
 introduced class names, so the −8.02 pp is not isolated to word order.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Blend normalized mean and variation directions into one vector per class, weighting by description count.

 RESULT
 Appended-bank 0.75 gate retained nothing on all three. At 0.90, relative to appended mean: EuroSAT −0.28 pp,
 CIFAR-100 +0.62 pp, RESISC45 +0.03 pp. LME on the same appended bank scored 51.59%, 51.58%, 58.96% vs blend
 51.20%, 51.10%, 58.90%.

 INTERPRETATION
 A single vector shifts every image score in the same way for that class, whereas LME can favor an
 image-specific prototype. On EuroSAT only River and Sea or Lake vectors moved; River gained 17 correct but
 Forest lost 26 through class competition. The RESISC45 difference is only a few images. No downstream blend
 retraining.

Anchor selection and retrieval (DINO teacher only)
Hypotheses, measured results, and failure modes; stages and splits are kept separate.



 HYPOTHESIS
 Confidence-gated DINO coverage swaps replace redundant anchors.

 RESULT
 Tiny EuroSAT pilot on 240 images gained +5, +2, +9 correct across three seeds.

 INTERPRETATION
 Worth scaling, not independent datasets or untouched splits; same small development rows.


 HYPOTHESIS
 At matched anchor counts, choose farthest-first, medoids, or agreement with visual neighbors.

 RESULT
 Teacher validation (confidence / farthest / medoids / agreement): EuroSAT 67.07 / 61.48 / 62.07 / 69.74%;
 RESISC45 69.32 / 66.38 / 67.16 / 69.59%; CIFAR-100 coarse 63.98 / 62.20 / 62.38 / 62.80%.

 INTERPRETATION
 Farthest-first and medoids lost everywhere. Agreement raised anchor precision across datasets, but teacher
 gain was clear only on EuroSAT, and CIFAR declined. Agreement alone does not ensure useful coverage.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Cluster-first rescue with top-2/3 CLIP constraints.

 RESULT
 EuroSAT baseline 67.06%, plain expansion 67.81%, DINO cluster-first 67.83%. All 113 additions went to Pasture;
 zero Herbaceous passed the gate, and Herbaceous recall was 2/600 at baseline and with cluster-first.

 INTERPRETATION
 The aggregate gain was not a rescue of the intended class. Candidate/gate restrictions mattered more than the
 clustering here.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Relax the candidate pool, add 64 anchors each to Herbaceous and Pasture, compare no-cluster retrieval and
 clustering.

 RESULT
 EuroSAT baseline 67.06%; CLIP expansion 67.28%, spherical K-means 67.52%, Ward 67.93%, direct DINO seed
 retrieval 68.02%, DBSCAN 68.28%. Direct retrieval found 96/128 correct additions (CLIP 79/128) and Herbaceous
 recall 98/600 (baseline 2/600; CLIP expansion 72/600).

 INTERPRETATION
 Best concrete rare-class rescue. DBSCAN had one dominant cluster and 6,810 noise points, not evidence of
 semantic subtypes. One dataset/seed; no zero-seed rescue tested here.

Anchor selection and retrieval (DINO teacher only)
Continued from the previous page.



 HYPOTHESIS
 Cluster-balance anchors and map visual clusters to text prototypes.

 RESULT
 Same EuroSAT budgets across seven arms. Cluster balance cut accuracy by 9.50–11.24 pp depending on bank.
 Prototype confidence 67.43% vs cluster-balanced 56.98%; mapping 57.09%, just +0.11 pp vs unmapped.

 INTERPRETATION
 Counting cluster coverage without preserving pseudo-label quality was costly; prototype mapping did not fix
 it.


 HYPOTHESIS
 DINO spectral clustering; reweight the same graph with CLIP posterior overlap.

 RESULT
 EuroSAT: CLIP expansion 67.28%, direct retrieval 68.02%, DINO spectral 67.52%, CLIP-weighted spectral 67.28%.
 CIFAR-100 simulated-scarcity pilot: 65.43%, 64.06%, 64.36%, 63.87% in the same order.

 INTERPRETATION
 Edge weighting did not improve spectral. CIFAR direct retrieval had 38/40 correct added anchors versus 28/40
 for CLIP but worse teacher accuracy. Purity is not enough; subtype diversity and representativeness are
 plausible factors, not established causes.


 HYPOTHESIS
 Stronger frozen retrieval encoder (public DINOv3 ConvNeXt-Tiny), then SigLIP 2 reranking × retrieval
 factorial.

 RESULT
 ConvNeXt with CLIP: EuroSAT 68.43% vs original DINO 68.02%; CIFAR scarcity pilot 64.84% vs 64.06%, still below
 CLIP-only expansion 65.43%. Factorial (CLIP+DINO / SigLIP+DINO / CLIP+ConvNeXt / SigLIP+ConvNeXt): EuroSAT
 68.02 / 68.57 / 68.43 / 68.83%; CIFAR pilot 64.06 / 64.55 / 64.84 / 64.65%.

 INTERPRETATION
 SigLIP increased correct additions in all matched swaps but not teacher accuracy in every pairing. CLIP still
 nominated candidates and seeds, so no full replacement was tested. Gated DINOv3 ViT checkpoint returned HTTP
 401; public ConvNeXt was a separate tested backbone, not a result for the blocked ViT.

APPENDIX / EXPERIMENT INVENTORY


Current judgment and next hypothesis
Hypotheses, measured results, and failure modes; stages and splits are kept separate.



 1. Keep the released single-mean CDE and confidence-based anchors as the cross-dataset baseline. Do not
 promote a template, threshold, or clustering rule just because it won one development run.


 2. Most promising mechanism: a class needs correct anchors across its visual modes. Missing nominations cannot
 be fixed by reranking the same candidates, and adding correct but redundant anchors can still harm
 generalization. Measure candidate availability, correct anchors per class/subtype, teacher accuracy, and
 student accuracy independently.



 3. Best next discriminating experiment: compare CLIP-only vs SigLIP 2-only nomination and ranking with the
 same DINO retrieval/head, matched realized per-class budgets, fresh validation or multiple seeds, and no test
 tuning. Include the existing CLIP-pool/SigLIP-rerank control to separate better ranking from finding examples
 CLIP missed. Track time and class coverage. Only advance to matched student training if the teacher gain
 repeats. A secondary, cheaper check is satellite suffix mean versus released CDE at matched anchor budgets;
 that isolates whether the observed teacher gain was just a different allocation of anchors.



 Technical issues (not scientific failures): an import namespace collision in a local pilot was worked around
 before the successful EuroSAT run; public archive transfers timed out/reset, and the RESISC45 archive was
 resumed and hash-checked. A float32 grouping consistency check differed by 1.69e-7; moving the check to
 float64 recovered the run without changing classifier math. No score was inferred for unfinished runs.

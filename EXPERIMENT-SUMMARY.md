# NoLA experiment summary

Scope: the locally documented NoLA walkthrough and follow-up studies. The older frozen Kaggle/DNC study is separate; this is not a fresh audit of that study. CDE means the frozen CLIP class-description classifier; alignment head means the fitted head on frozen DINO features; prompt classifier means visual-prompt tuning of CLIP. Compare numbers only inside the same split, stage, and experiment. Most follow-ups are development validation, often single-seed. Oxford Pets used its test split for a prompt comparison; do not treat a later reuse of those rows as untouched confirmation.

## 1. Baseline, scale, and what fails

| Experiment / hypothesis | Result | Interpretation / what went wrong |
|---|---|---|
| EuroSAT small pipeline and 160/320-image sweep: more data or more anchors should help. | 160-image trace: 35 candidates → 34 distinct anchors, 25 correct; Herbaceous had no CLIP top-1 candidates and needed fallback. In the separate four-cell sweep, CDE was 117/240, DINO 142–159/240, prompt classifier 81–140/240. Raising the cap from 4 to 12 added anchors, reduced precision, improved the alignment head in both matched rows, and hurt the prompt classifier. At 320 images one class lost coverage. | More images or anchors do not guarantee class coverage; fallback and deduplication matter. Higher alignment head accuracy did not automatically reach the prompt classifier. |
| Larger small-data hyperparameter grid: tune head and prompt classifier to remove the gap. | 10 of 128 configurations are saved, 20 epoch checkpoints. Best saved prompt classifier 143/240, tied with its alignment head; prompt classifiers ranged from 59 to 143 correct. | Partial, single-seed grid; no defensible global winner. Epochs and batch size also change the number of updates. |
| Instrumented EuroSAT CPU replay and DINO-space audit: find the specific failure. | On 240 validation images: CDE 117, alignment head 134, prompt classifier 87 at epoch 1 and 124 at epoch 4. Fresh 240-image subset: 130, 137, and 121 at epoch 4. Herbaceous: 24 true train examples, zero top-1 nominations, one wrong fallback, 0/16 validation correct throughout. Pasture had no correct anchors; Permanent Crop had four wrong anchors. Two of nine supported predicted-class DINO regions lacked anchors; only 3/7 and 1/4 of their rows had correct CLIP labels. | An absent nomination is different from low anchor precision. Pure diversity may give more slots to coherent *wrong* labels. At epoch 4 the prompt classifier fixed 24 alignment head errors and lost 34 correct alignment head predictions. |
| Full-split EuroSAT local release-style baseline: is NoLA itself viable at scale? | CDE 51.09%, alignment head 67.09%, best prompt classifier 76.69% validation (epoch 3); selected checkpoint 76.27% test (6,178/8,100). Epoch 50 fell to 71.13% validation. | End-to-end NoLA worked well in this run. Checkpoint selection mattered; small pilots that lost to DINO do not establish that the prompt classifier generally fails. Local release-style run, not an exact multi-seed paper reproduction. |

Sources: `NoLA-EuroSAT-{Pipeline,Sweep,Full-Sweep,Run-It-Yourself}.ipynb`, `eurosat-full-sweep.jsonl`, `eurosat-case-study/{metrics.json,weakness-audit/README.md}`.

## 2. Text-side experiments (CDE)

| Experiment / hypothesis | Result | Interpretation / what went wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Three prompt prototypes via K-means instead of one class mean. | EuroSAT 240-image pilot: mean 118, log-mean-exp (LME) 127, max 103; Herbaceous 0/16 in all arms. | Soft pooling looked promising on a reused small set; max was worse, consistent with an individual mismatched prompt dominating. No evidence of rare-class rescue. |
| Compress authored prompts via complete-link cosine; use SVD as a geometry diagnostic. | On the other 5,160 EuroSAT validation images: mean 53.59%; unmerged 100 descriptions 54.28%; 36/57/72 merged prototypes 48.90/48.39/49.42%. SVD projection marked some pairs as close that failed full-space cosine; they were not merged on that basis. | Merging can alter both representation and per-description weights; the losses do not isolate information loss. SVD directions are not subtype counts. |
| Broad descriptions plus named or unnamed variations should recover semantic coverage. | Same 5,160 rows: broad-only 47.67%, best broad-plus-variants 50.21%, below 53.59% mean and 54.28% unmerged controls. | Adding variants helped that weak broad bank, not the strongest matched prompt controls. Explicit class naming was not a guarantee of improvement. |
| Merge the released EuroSAT bank rather than the newly authored bank. | Same 5,160 rows: original released CDE 51.10%; descriptions-only centroid 50.79%; unmerged released descriptions 51.96%; best merge (0.80) 52.29%, 0.75 merge 52.15%. Herbaceous still had zero correct. | Some prompt compression worked for this bank, but the result did not transfer across banks or solve the main class failure. |
| Photo-wrap each description, then cluster at 0.75 and use in NoLA. | EuroSAT corrected Colab arm (16 prototypes): alignment head 3,624→3,679/5,400; prompt classifier epoch 1 +1.15 pp and epoch 2 −0.30 pp. No verified final test result for this comparison. RESISC45: 50 descriptions/class → 123 total prototypes; frozen CDE 58.03→56.05%. | prompt classifier advantage reversed by epoch; across datasets even the CDE effect reversed. An earlier unwrapped author-scale arm was superseded, not a completed comparison. |
| Keep the mean and add only sufficiently different clusters with separate LME components. | Wrapped-bank 0.75 gate retained 0/16 EuroSAT clusters and 0/27 CIFAR-100 coarse clusters. Exact mean parity: 51.56% and 58.50%. CIFAR gate 0.90 activated 7 clusters: 58.36% (−0.14 pp, interval includes zero). | An inactive gate tests the mean, not active variants. More permissive gating did not show a gain on that CIFAR bank. |
| Append the class template to each description, with a single mean vector. | Frozen CDE: Oxford Pets 85.61→87.90% (3,669, our one-template control); EuroSAT 51.06→51.48%; RESISC45 58.07→58.86%; CIFAR-100 coarse 58.50→50.48% versus previous photo-wrapped prompts. | Favorable for some domain/breed wording; not a universal formatting rule. CIFAR also changed wording and introduced class names, so the −8.02 pp is not isolated to word order. |
| Blend normalized mean and variation directions into one vector per class, weighting by description count. | Appended-bank 0.75 gate retained nothing on all three. At 0.90, relative to appended mean: EuroSAT −0.28 pp, CIFAR-100 +0.62 pp, RESISC45 +0.03 pp. LME on the same appended bank scored 51.59%, 51.58%, 58.96% vs blend 51.20%, 51.10%, 58.90%. | A single vector shifts every image score in the same way for that class, whereas LME can favor an image-specific prototype. On EuroSAT only River and Sea or Lake vectors moved; River gained 17 correct but Forest lost 26 through class competition. The RESISC45 difference is only a few images. No downstream blend retraining. |
| Does the suffix alone help after training? Local EuroSAT matched runs. | 300-image pilot test, baseline→suffix: CDE 49.67→48.67%; alignment head 64.00→64.33%; prompt classifier 69.33→67.33%. Full split: alignment head test 66.96→68.90%, Herbaceous anchors 7→21; prompt classifier validation 74.33→72.33% at epoch 1, 73.15→74.74% at epoch 2. No final paired prompt classifier test export found for the full split. | alignment head gains and better class coverage did not imply stable prompt classifier gains. Both full-split arms reached their two validation milestones; a completed paired prompt classifier test is not established. CIFAR-10 archive was verified, but the local log stops at waiting for the GPU; no verified CIFAR-10 suffix result found. |

## 3. Anchor selection and retrieval (DINO alignment head only)

| Experiment / hypothesis | Result | Interpretation / what went wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Confidence-gated DINO coverage swaps replace redundant anchors. | Tiny EuroSAT pilot on 240 images gained +5, +2, +9 correct across three seeds. | Worth scaling, not independent datasets or untouched splits; same small development rows. |
| At matched anchor counts, choose farthest-first, medoids, or agreement with visual neighbors. | alignment head validation (confidence / farthest / medoids / agreement): EuroSAT 67.07 / 61.48 / 62.07 / 69.74%; RESISC45 69.32 / 66.38 / 67.16 / 69.59%; CIFAR-100 coarse 63.98 / 62.20 / 62.38 / 62.80%. | Farthest-first and medoids lost everywhere. Agreement raised anchor precision across datasets, but alignment head gain was clear only on EuroSAT, and CIFAR declined. Agreement alone does not ensure useful coverage. |
| Cluster-first rescue with top-2/3 CLIP constraints. | EuroSAT baseline 67.06%, plain expansion 67.81%, DINO cluster-first 67.83%. All 113 additions went to Pasture; zero Herbaceous passed the gate, and Herbaceous recall was 2/600 at baseline and with cluster-first. | The aggregate gain was not a rescue of the intended class. Candidate/gate restrictions mattered more than the clustering here. |
| Relax the candidate pool, add 64 anchors each to Herbaceous and Pasture, compare no-cluster retrieval and clustering. | EuroSAT baseline 67.06%; CLIP expansion 67.28%, spherical K-means 67.52%, Ward 67.93%, direct DINO seed retrieval 68.02%, DBSCAN 68.28%. Direct retrieval found 96/128 correct additions (CLIP 79/128) and Herbaceous recall 98/600 (baseline 2/600; CLIP expansion 72/600). | Best concrete rare-class rescue. DBSCAN had one dominant cluster and 6,810 noise points, not evidence of semantic subtypes. One dataset/seed; no zero-seed rescue tested here. |
| Cluster-balance anchors and map visual clusters to text prototypes. | Same EuroSAT budgets across seven arms. Cluster balance cut accuracy by 9.50–11.24 pp depending on bank. Prototype confidence 67.43% vs cluster-balanced 56.98%; mapping 57.09%, just +0.11 pp vs unmapped. | Counting cluster coverage without preserving pseudo-label quality was costly; prototype mapping did not fix it. |
| DINO spectral clustering; reweight the same graph with CLIP posterior overlap. | EuroSAT: CLIP expansion 67.28%, direct retrieval 68.02%, DINO spectral 67.52%, CLIP-weighted spectral 67.28%. CIFAR-100 simulated-scarcity pilot: 65.43%, 64.06%, 64.36%, 63.87% in the same order. | Edge weighting did not improve spectral. CIFAR direct retrieval had 38/40 correct added anchors versus 28/40 for CLIP but worse alignment head accuracy. Purity is not enough; subtype diversity and representativeness are plausible factors, not established causes. |
| Stronger frozen retrieval encoder (public DINOv3 ConvNeXt-Tiny), then SigLIP 2 reranking × retrieval factorial. | ConvNeXt with CLIP: EuroSAT 68.43% vs original DINO 68.02%; CIFAR scarcity pilot 64.84% vs 64.06%, still below CLIP-only expansion 65.43%. Factorial (CLIP+DINO / SigLIP+DINO / CLIP+ConvNeXt / SigLIP+ConvNeXt): EuroSAT 68.02 / 68.57 / 68.43 / 68.83%; CIFAR pilot 64.06 / 64.55 / 64.84 / 64.65%. | SigLIP increased correct additions in all matched swaps but not alignment head accuracy in every pairing. CLIP still nominated candidates and seeds, so no full replacement was tested. Gated DINOv3 ViT checkpoint returned HTTP 401; public ConvNeXt was a separate tested backbone, not a result for the blocked ViT. |

## Current judgment and next hypothesis

1. Keep the released single-mean CDE and confidence-based anchors as the cross-dataset baseline. Do not promote a template, threshold, or clustering rule just because it won one development run.
2. Most promising mechanism: a class needs correct anchors across its visual modes. Missing nominations cannot be fixed by reranking the same candidates, and adding correct but redundant anchors can still harm generalization. Measure candidate availability, correct anchors per class/subtype, alignment head accuracy, and prompt classifier accuracy independently.
3. Independent replacement is now tested: see `siglip-replacement/README.md`. At matched budgets, SigLIP 2-only alignment head accuracy trails CLIP-only by 9.89 pp on EuroSAT and by 1.17 pp on the CIFAR-100 pilot (the latter interval includes zero). SigLIP nominates 144 correct EuroSAT Herbaceous examples at top-1 versus none from CLIP, yet selects fewer correct Herbaceous anchors with the matched budgets. Keep CLIP as default; the next discriminating check is how to rank candidates for that class, rather than another wholesale backbone swap. A secondary check is satellite suffix mean versus released CDE at matched anchor budgets.

Technical issues (not scientific failures): an import namespace collision in a local pilot was worked around before the successful EuroSAT run; public archive transfers timed out/reset, and the RESISC45 archive was resumed and hash-checked. A float32 grouping consistency check differed by 1.69e-7; moving the check to float64 recovered the run without changing classifier math. No score was inferred for unfinished runs.

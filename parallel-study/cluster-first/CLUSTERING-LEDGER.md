# Clustering algorithms for underfilled classes

Completed on the local GTX 1050 Ti. Reused frozen EuroSAT features (13,500 train / 5,400 validation), original CDE, and same 50-epoch head settings. No test scoring, no visual prompt training. All rescue arms added exactly 64 anchors per underfilled class (Herbaceous and Pasture), kept every original anchor, and used a single candidate pool with no top-three restriction. This study did not exercise zero-anchor rescue on the full split. Compared CLIP confidence, DINO contrastive seed retrieval without clustering, spherical k-means, Ward on train-only 32-dimensional PCA, and DBSCAN. Clustering provided a clipped leave-one-out score adjustment with small-cluster shrinkage, not a quota or gate. The no-rescue control remained at 2,324 anchors.

Commands in the research-lab venv: `clustering_sweep.py --smoke`, `clustering_sweep.py`, `verify_clustering.py clustering_sweep_eurosat`. Both real-data smoke and full reports were verified independently. Full report: `clustering_sweep_eurosat/verification.json`, alongside matched predictions, anchor audit, per-class CSV, cluster assignments, and settings.

DBSCAN reached 68.278% vs flat expansion 67.278% (paired 95% interval +0.389 to +1.611 pp). It returned a single dominant cluster (6,626 images), two smaller clusters, and 6,810 noise points; do not interpret the result as evidence of useful class variants. DINO seed retrieval selected 96/128 correct additions (most) and gave 98/600 Herbaceous recall (best); CLIP expansion gave 79/128 and 72/600. Single-seed, development validation; no evidence yet of cross-dataset repeatability.
